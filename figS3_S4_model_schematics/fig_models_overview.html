Choice / correctness; high or low confidence

→

Fit accuracy first

e = ks + σcη,   η ~ 𝒩(0,1)

Correct iff e > 0; fit k, σlo; fix σhi = 1

→

Reconstruct latent evidence

Sample |e| conditional on s, c, and outcome.

4,000 draws per cell; fixed during confidence fitting.

Below, p is the high-confidence probability for a latent draw. Average over draws to predict the observed report.

|e| + noise scale → normalized strength

1  Calibrated · 2 parameters

p = Φ(κ|e|/σc + b)

Adjust sensitivity and criterion.

Free: κ, b.

2  Bayesian(|e|) · 1 parameter

p = Φ(|e|/σc + b)

Known contrast-specific noise; shift the confidence criterion.

Free: b (κ fixed at 1).

3  ContrastBlind · 2 parameters

p = Φ(κ|e|/σpool + b)

One pooled noise scale at both contrasts.

Free: κ, b.

4  CASANDRE · 3 parameters

p = Φ(κ|e|/σ̂ + b)

σ̂ = σcexp(τz),   z ~ 𝒩(0,1)

A noisy estimate of the noise scale.

Free: κ, b, τ.

Infer a posterior from scalar e

e → P(h|e),   h = (sign, s, c)

↔ Route 2, Fig. S4
↓

5  FullBayes(e) · 1 parameter

q = P(chosen sign | e),   p = Φ(Φ−1(q) + b)

Sum joint-posterior mass for the chosen sign; marginalize over magnitude and contrast; shift its criterion in probit space.

Free: b.

6  Entropy(e) · 2 parameters

H− = ∑h P(h|e) log P(h|e)

p = Φ(κH− + b)

Summarize concentration of the entire posterior;

do not select mass for the chosen sign.

Free: κ, b.

Both use the same joint hypothesis grid.
Flat priors over sign and 40 magnitude bins; empirical prior over contrast. The likelihood uses the parameters from the shared accuracy fit.

Use reconstructed chosen/unchosen pools

(|e|, gc) → E+, E− → p

↔ Route 3, Fig. S4
↓

7  PositiveEvidence/Gain · 3 identified parameters

(|e|, gc) → E+, E− → p

E+ = (gc+|e|)/2,   E− = (gc−|e|)/2

p = Φ(κ[E+ − αE−] + b) = Φ(Q|e| + Bc)

Pools are reconstructed, not measured.

Nominal: κ, b, glo, α (fix ghi=1); α=tanh(θα)∈(−1,1).

Only Q, Bhi, Blo are identified (box below) — these 3 are what is fit and reported.

Only 3 combinations are identifiable:
p = Φ(Q|e| + Bc)
Q = κ(1+α)/2
Bc = b + κ(1−α)gc/2

κ, b, glo, α enter the likelihood only through this product — any α has an exactly compensating glo that reproduces the identical fit.

The data do not uniquely identify α (or glo) separately.
Reading the figure

Φ: standard normal CDF. κ: confidence sensitivity; b: confidence offset. σpool = √((σhi²+σlo²)/2).

Counts refer to confidence parameters beyond the shared accuracy fit. Every model here has at least one free confidence parameter (bare zero-parameter ideal-observer versions of Bayesian(|e|) and FullBayes(e) were considered but are not part of the reported model set).
